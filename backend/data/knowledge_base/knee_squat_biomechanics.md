# Knee Joint Stress, Squat Biomechanics & Pain Mitigation

## Evidence Source
- Schoenfeld, B. J. (2010). Squatting kinematics and kinetics and their application to exercise performance. Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research.
- Escamilla, R. F. (2001). Knee biomechanics of the dynamic squat exercise. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise.

## Core Recommendations
1. **Patellofemoral Joint Force**:
   - Patellofemoral compressive force increases progressively with knee flexion angle, peaking near 90-100 degrees of flexion.
   - For lifters experiencing anterior knee or patellar tendon pain:
     - Substitute barbell back squats with Box Squats or Pin Squats to minimize anterior tibial translation.
     - Employ a moderate shins-vertical cue or hip-dominant movement (such as Romanian Deadlifts or Bulgarian Split Squats with a forward torso lean).

2. **Exercise Substitutions for Knee Irritation**:
   - Acute Phase: Reverse Nordics, Spanish Squats (with isometric band support), and deficit reverse lunges.
   - Movement adjustments: Increase stance width slightly (1.2-1.4x shoulder width) with ~20-30 degrees of outward foot flare to reduce lateral knee torque.

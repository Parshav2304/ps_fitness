# Shoulder Impingement & Overhead Press Biomechanics

## Evidence Source
- Cools, A. M., et al. (2014). Rehabilitation of scapular dyskinesis: from the office worker to the elite overhead athlete. British Journal of Sports Medicine.
- Reinold, M. M., et al. (2009). Current concepts in the scientific and practical approach to shoulder rehabilitation. Journal of Orthopaedic & Sports Physical Therapy.

## Core Recommendations
1. **Subacromial Impingement Mechanics**:
   - Pure frontal plane abduction (flared elbows during overhead pressing) reduces the subacromial space, compressing the supraspinatus tendon.
   - Pressing in the **Scapular Plane** (30 degrees anterior to the frontal plane) maximizes subacromial clearance and optimizes serratus anterior activation.

2. **Substitutions for Anterior Shoulder Pinch**:
   - Neutral-Grip Dumbbell Overhead Press: Elbows tucked forward at 45 degrees.
   - Half-Kneeling Landmine Press: Provides an angled arc of motion (~60-70 degrees) avoiding terminal end-range impingement.
   - Warm-up Protocol: External rotator cuff activation (Face Pulls, Cuban rotations) and thoracic spine extension over a foam roller prior to pressing.

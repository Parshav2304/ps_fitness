# Hypertrophy Volume & Progressive Overload Thresholds

## Evidence Source
- Schoenfeld, B. J., Ogborn, D., & Krieger, J. W. (2017/2021). Dose-response relationship between weekly resistance training volume and increases in muscle mass: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Sports Sciences.

## Core Recommendations
1. **Weekly Set Volume**:
   - Minimum Effective Dose (MED): ~6-8 hard sets per muscle group per week for maintenance/modest gains.
   - Maximum Adaptive Volume (MAV): 10-20 hard sets per muscle group per week for optimal hypertrophic gains.
   - Maximum Recoverable Volume (MRV): Beyond 22-25 sets/week typically yields diminishing returns and systemic fatigue.

2. **Proximity to Failure (RIR / RPE)**:
   - Sets should generally be terminated at 1-3 Reps in Reserve (RIR 1-3 or RPE 7-9) on heavy compound lifts (squats, bench, deadlifts).
   - Isolation lifts (bicep curls, lateral raises, leg extensions) can safely be taken to 0 RIR (RPE 10) on the final set.

3. **Progressive Overload Modalities**:
   - Double Progression: First increase reps within a target bracket (e.g., 8-12 reps). Once the upper target is hit across all sets, increase load by 2.5-5% and reset reps to the bottom of the bracket.

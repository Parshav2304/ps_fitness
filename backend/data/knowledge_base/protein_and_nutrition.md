# Protein Requirements & Macro Distribution Guidelines

## Evidence Source
- Jäger, R., Kerksick, C. M., Campbell, B. I., et al. (2017/2023). International Society of Sports Nutrition Position Stand: Protein and Exercise. Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition.

## Core Recommendations
1. **Total Daily Protein Intake**:
   - For athletes engaged in intense resistance training seeking hypertrophy or recomposition: 1.6 to 2.2 g/kg of body weight per day (0.73 - 1.0 g/lb).
   - In a severe caloric deficit (cutting phase with body fat < 12% in men, < 20% in women): 2.0 to 2.4 g/kg/day to maximize lean tissue preservation.

2. **Per-Meal Protein Distribution**:
   - Optimal acute anabolic response occurs at ~0.40 to 0.55 g/kg/meal of high-quality protein (providing 2.5-3.5g of leucine), spaced evenly across 3 to 5 meals per day (every 3 to 5 hours).

3. **Carbohydrate and Fat Minimums**:
   - Carbohydrates: Crucial for glycogen replenishment and central nervous system stamina. Recommend at least 3.0-5.0 g/kg/day for moderate to high volume training.
   - Dietary Fats: Never drop below 0.5-0.6 g/kg/day or 15-20% of total calories to maintain healthy endocrine/testosterone production.
